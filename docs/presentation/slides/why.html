Background and aim

  • Benchmarking a variant caller requires variants known to be present in realistic data.
  • Carriers of a given pathogenic variant are rare, and seldom sequenced with the laboratory's own protocol.
  • Fully simulated reads lack the sample's depth profile, error profile, library and alignment behaviour.
  • Aim: modify only the reads overlapping a chosen variant in a real sample, and record exactly what was planted.

spike · Introduction · 2 / 25

The problem spike addresses: to validate a caller you need variants you know are there, in data that looks like your own. Real carriers are rare; fully simulated data is too clean. spike keeps everything about a real sample except the variant you plant. Figure 1 is a drawing of the idea, not data: the synthetic reads that cross the deleted interval are the split reads a caller looks for.

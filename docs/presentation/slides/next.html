Limitations and next steps

Limitations

  • Crashed 3′ ends about a quarter short (Figure 13); Q25 and Q37 bases err more often than real ones (slide 11)
  • Soft clips other than bad ends (adapters, chimeras, foreign sequence) are not made
  • No synthetic duplicate reads
  • One depth scales each event; the local departure is recorded, not corrected
  • In low-mappability regions uneditable reads remain; --edit-model origin is experimental
  • Results shown: one sample (HG002, 35×), chromosome 20, BAM route

Next steps

  • Whole-sample FASTQ runs through nf-core/raredisease at the hospital
  • The 37 ClinVar structural variants in LDLR, one at a time, through each SV caller
  • Repeat the carrier comparison (Table 7) with the new quality model
  • Duplications, SNVs and indels through the hospital pipeline (not yet run)

The measurements so far are preliminary validation, meant to catch problems before the real runs, which will be whole FASTQ sets through the complete raredisease pipeline at the hospital. The quality model was rebuilt in October 2026: reads now differ from one another as real reads do, and crashed ends and their soft clips come close to the sample's rates. What remains is smaller: crashes a quarter short, high-quality bases erring somewhat too often, and the soft clips that have other causes, which spike does not reproduce.

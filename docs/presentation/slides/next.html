Limitations and next steps

Limitations

  • Quality varies too little from read to read: almost no perfect or poor reads (Figure 12)
  • Crashed 3′ ends and soft clips are under-represented (Figure 13, Table 7)
  • No synthetic duplicate reads
  • One depth scales each event; the local departure is recorded, not corrected
  • In low-mappability regions uneditable reads remain; --edit-model origin is experimental
  • Results shown: one sample (HG002, 35×), chromosome 20, BAM route

Next steps

  • Whole-sample FASTQ runs through nf-core/raredisease at the hospital
  • The 37 ClinVar structural variants in LDLR, one at a time, through each SV caller
  • An error model for crashed read ends, estimated from donor reads
  • Duplications, SNVs and indels through the hospital pipeline (not yet run)

The measurements so far are preliminary validation, meant to catch problems before the real runs, which will be whole FASTQ sets through the complete raredisease pipeline at the hospital. The main modelling gap is the quality model's one-base memory: real reads are good or poor as a whole, spike's are all average, and the low-quality tail of real reads is one sign of it; a first fix restored the crash rate but not the soft clipping, because real crashed ends carry many more errors than their quality scores imply.

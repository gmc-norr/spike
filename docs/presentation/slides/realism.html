An independent check: deletions that HG001 really carries were planted into HG002, and spike's reads were compared read by read with HG001's real reads at the same places. Depth, clipping positions at the breakpoints, loss of heterozygosity and alignment tags agreed. With the old quality model, crashed read ends and the soft clips that follow from them were far rarer than in real reads; the new model brings both close to the sample's rates, measured in other tests. Duplicates are still not made. The carrier comparison should be repeated with the new model.

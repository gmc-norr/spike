Allele fraction: requested versus measured

  • 25 SNVs, 60 kb apart on chr20, five each at f = 0.1, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75 and 1.0, in one run
  • 25–55 voting fragments per site
  • Requested f lies inside the site's 95% CI at 22 of 25 sites. Two others are at f = 1 (31 of 32 and 37 of 39 fragments), where any non-alt fragment puts 1.0 outside; one is at f = 0.75 (21 of 35)
  • 20 of 20 graded sites pass the validate test; the five at f = 0.1 are too shallow to grade

Twenty-five SNVs planted in one run at five allele fractions, then aligned, merged and counted. The error bars are binomial confidence intervals from each site's own fragment count, so the scatter around the diagonal can be read against sampling noise at this depth. The requested fraction falls inside the interval at 22 of 25 sites, about what 95 percent intervals allow. Two exceptions are homozygous sites with 31 of 32 and 37 of 39 fragments carrying the variant: at a requested fraction of 1, a single fragment without it is enough to exclude 1.0. The third is a site at 0.75 measured at 21 of 35. All 20 graded sites pass the validate test.

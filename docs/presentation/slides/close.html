Summary

  • spike plants structural variants, SNVs and indels into a real sample's reads and records the truth
  • Each random component uses a model estimated from the sample's own reads: empirical resampling, a first-order Markov chain for base quality, Phred-scaled errors
  • In real runs, depth, allele fractions, fragment lengths and quality transitions agree with expectation and with real reads
  • Known gap: quality varies too little from read to read; long low-quality runs at the 3′ end, and the soft clips they cause, are rarer than in real reads

spike --bam sample.bam --reference GRCh38.fasta --event "del:chr17:43045000-43055000" -o out/

bash out/align.sh && bash out/merge.sh

spike validate --bam out/merged.bam --truth out/truth.vcf --reference GRCh38.fasta

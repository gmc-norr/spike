Summary

  • spike plants structural variants, SNVs and indels into a real sample's reads and records the truth
  • Each random component uses a model estimated from the sample's own reads: empirical resampling for fragments, and a quality context model and error table learned from a sample of the input
  • In real runs, depth, allele fractions, fragment lengths, read-to-read quality spread and crashed 3′ ends agree with expectation and with real reads
  • Known gaps: crashed ends about a quarter short; soft clips with other causes, and duplicates, are not made

spike --bam sample.bam --reference GRCh38.fasta --event "del:chr17:43045000-43055000" -o out/

bash out/align.sh && bash out/merge.sh

spike validate --bam out/merged.bam --truth out/truth.vcf --reference GRCh38.fasta
